Professional Focus

My professional interests are focused on the areas described throughout this page: C++, native software development, systems programming, graphics programming, compilers, runtimes, performance-oriented software, engineering tools, and related low-level technologies.

If you are contacting me regarding a professional opportunity, please make sure the position is substantially related to these areas.

I am not interested in web development, front-end or back-end web positions, web services, database-oriented roles, Android development, Selenium, browser automation, web-oriented QA/testing, cloud infrastructure positions, AWS-oriented roles, or general Java enterprise/web development.

Software engineering covers a very broad range of disciplines. Being an experienced software engineer does not mean that every software position is relevant to my professional interests. My career direction is deliberately centered on the engineering areas presented on this site.

Testing and validation work is of interest to me when it is closely related to systems, hardware, runtimes, compilers, graphics, native platforms, or other low-level engineering. I am not looking for general web QA or browser-based testing positions.

I also have substantial experience with mainframe technologies, z/OS, HLASM, and related low-level mainframe development. I consider these technologies technically interesting and I value what I learned while working with them. However, I have decided not to pursue any further professional work in the mainframe area.

If an opportunity is not reasonably aligned with the technical work, interests, and direction described on this Information page, please do not contact me regarding that position.

For relevant opportunities, I am always happy to have a serious technical conversation.
